How the twelve weeks unfold

Weeks 1–2

Ground

Movement screen, strength benchmarks, sleep & food audit. We map where you actually are — no judgment, just data.

Weeks 3–6

Build

Progressive strength blocks, the ten-meal kitchen system, and your first recovery protocol take root.

Weeks 7–10

Deepen

Load goes up, habits go automatic. Breathwork enters, deload week teaches you what rest can do.

Weeks 11–12

Integrate

Re-test everything. Then we design your independent system — the one you run without me.

About your coach

Hej, I'm Max.

Twelve years ago I was the coach handing out beach-body programs — six-week shreds, joyless meal plans, clients who vanished by autumn. It worked on paper and failed in lives. So I rebuilt my entire practice around one question: what does it take to still be strong, mobile and glad to move at seventy?

The answer became a method — strength training that respects your joints, food you cook because it tastes good, and recovery treated as seriously as work. I've refined it with over 140 clients across Stockholm: founders, new parents, former athletes, people starting from their first push-up.

You won't find before-and-after photos here. You'll find people who, three years later, are still training — because it finally fits their life.
