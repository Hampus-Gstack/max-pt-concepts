Philosophy

Three principles. Nothing else.

Most training fails from complexity, not effort. Everything I program traces back to three ideas — and if a session doesn't serve one of them, it gets cut.

01

Movement quality

Position before load, always. We earn the right to go heavy by owning each pattern first — squat, hinge, press, pull, carry. It's slower for two weeks and faster for ten years.

02

Consistency over intensity

The best program is the one you're still on in March. I build training around your actual week — travel, kids, deadlines — so the default outcome is showing up, not starting over.

03

Measured progress

Every lift, session and check-in is logged. We review the trend line together each month — so progress is something you can point to, not something you have to feel on a good day.

About

FAQ

Fair questions

Anything else — ask on the intro call. It's twenty minutes and costs nothing.

Do I need training experience to start?+

No. About half my clients start as complete beginners. The first four weeks are built around learning positions at light loads, so inexperience is an advantage — there's nothing to unlearn.

Where do sessions take place?+

In-person sessions run from a private studio in Östermalm, Stockholm, weekdays 06:00–19:00. Online clients train wherever they are — I program around the equipment you actually have.

How long until I see results?+

Strength numbers typically move within 3–4 weeks; visible change usually follows between weeks 8 and 12. I'll never promise a date — but you'll see the trend on paper long before you see it in the mirror, which is exactly why we log everything.

What does the intro call involve?+

Twenty minutes, no obligation. We talk through your history, your week and your goal. If I think I can help, I'll say how and recommend a format. If I can't, I'll point you to someone who can.
