Manifesto

The Standard

Four rules. Non-negotiable. If they sound harsh, this isn't for you — and that's fine.

  1. Show up. Every session.

    Consistency beats intensity, talent and every supplement you've ever bought. Miss a session and we reschedule it — we never delete it.

  2. Track everything. Guess nothing.

    Every lift, every meal window, every night of sleep is data. We don't argue with feelings — we read the log and adjust.

  3. Recovery is training.

    The work only counts if you absorb it. Sleep, food and deloads are programmed with the same precision as your heaviest week.

  4. Earn the next block.

    Progression is unlocked, not scheduled. You move on when the standard is met — under load, on camera, no rounding up.
