# Max — Content System (v2, simplified)
*Cursus Capital · 5 Sep 2026 · source for /iron/content/*

One line: **7 formats. One film block a week. We do the rest.**

How it runs (the only process Max needs to know):
1. **Sunday 18:00** — Cursus sends the week's shooting schedule (WhatsApp + PDF).
2. **Tuesday 10:30–12:00** — Max films the block. Uploads raw clips to the shared Drive folder.
3. **Tuesday–Wednesday** — Cursus edits, subtitles, writes captions. Max gets a preview Wednesday; a thumbs-up or one line of changes.
4. **Thursday** — the week is scheduled. Friday: numbers into the calendar.

## 1. The 7 formats

Each format is deconstructed the same way: **Hook (0–3s) → Body → CTA**, plus the exact shots it needs. Example posts are real, pulled 5 Sep 2026 with their engagement.

### F1 · THE REINTRODUCTION — purpose: trust · cadence: quarterly, pinned
**Example:** Harry Mackarness (coach), "Allow me to reintroduce myself" — https://www.instagram.com/resurgent_harry/p/DXQ6O_gjP4a/ — 4.3K likes / 75 comments, his top post of 2026. Text on a portrait, full origin story in the caption.
**Structure (60–90s reel, or a 5-slide carousel):**
- 0–3s HOOK — a line that contradicts what you see: "Most people cross the street when they see me. My clients are women in their fifties."
- 3–30s WHO — four facts, one line each: tattooed · licensed PT · 140+ women coached · 20 clients max.
- 30–60s WHY — the turn: why women 40+. One concrete moment, not a speech: his first client over fifty, terrified of the gym, deadlifting her bodyweight a year later. The past gets one calm sentence here, at most — "Before coaching, years in security work abroad. He left that life; this one is quieter." Never on screen.
- 60–80s PROMISE — what coaching is like: weekly video call, programme in the app, daily check-ins. Private, calm, no shouting.
- last 5s CTA — "If that's the kind of coach you want in your corner, the link in bio is a 20-minute call."
**Shots:** 4 × 10s talking head (his gym or home gym, seated, tattoos visible) · 3s cinematic empty gym · 5s hands loading a bar · 3s Max checking a training log · 1 still portrait (cover).
**On screen:** the hook line only. **Sound:** voice + low music.
**Max's hooks:** "Most people cross the street when they see me. My clients are women in their fifties." · "Allow me to reintroduce myself."

### F2 · THE LIST — purpose: reach · cadence: every 2 weeks
**Example:** Paul Dickinson, "Social media bullshit that needs to f*** right off" — https://www.instagram.com/barbells_and_mountains/reel/DbY3Lsiuf5l/ — 25.9K likes / 1.7K comments on a 32K-follower account. Numbered list on screen, caption is just "What have I missed?"
**Structure (45–60s):**
- 0–3s HOOK — title on screen + spoken: "Fitness advice for women over 40 that needs to go in the bin."
- 3–50s FIVE ITEMS — ~8s each. The number appears, one sentence, a beat of silence. Never explain twice.
- last 5s CTA — "What have I missed?" (the audience finishes the list — that is where the comments come from).
**Shots:** one 50s walking take (a park or street near him, phone at chest height, 0.5× lens, two takes) — or static in the rack. No B-roll; the text does the work.
**Item bank:** "toning" · light weights forever · cardio as punishment · "you're too old to start" · the 1,200-calorie diet · daily weigh-ins · "just do Pilates".
**Guardrail:** contrarian about the industry, never about her.

### F3 · THEN / NOW — purpose: trust · personality · cadence: every 2 weeks
**Example:** Shane Dorian, "2 steaks a night" — https://www.instagram.com/doriancoreperformance/reel/Db5j6tlIw_H/ — 4.1K likes / 101 comments. What he did in his twenties vs how he trains now, ending on why. A personal then/now that lands on a lesson.
**Structure (45–60s):**
- 0–3s HOOK — the "then" as one line: "At 25 I trained to look dangerous."
- 3–30s THEN — what he did and why. One concrete detail. Allowed to be funny.
- 30–50s NOW — what changed and why it matters for her: "Now I train so I can carry my daughter up four flights at seventy. So do my clients."
- CTA — soft: "Follow for the next one."
**Shots:** 3 × 10s talking head (his gym or home gym) or one 40s walking take · 3s cinematic detail (training log, chalk, the gym door) · 5s low-angle of his own lift as the "now" visual.
**Max's hooks:** "At 25 I trained to look dangerous. At 40 I train to be useful." · "Two hours a day in my twenties. Three sessions a week now. Stronger."
**Guardrail:** his past is backstory, one sentence at most, never the hook.

### F4 · ONE MINUTE — purpose: consistency · cadence: 3 per week, batch-filmed
**Example:** Xavier The Life Coach, "I almost gave up on my Instagram" — https://www.instagram.com/xavierthelifecoach/reel/Dbdep8-Iwi2/ — 5.4K likes / 752 comments. One thought, to camera, honest, 60 seconds. His whole account is this format.
**Structure (45–60s):**
- 0–3s HOOK — the thought as one sentence: "You don't need motivation. You need a Tuesday at seven that's already booked."
- 3–45s ONE IDEA — why, in plain words, one example. No second point.
- 45–55s LANDING — say the line again.
- CTA — none, or "save this".
**Shots:** 60s talking head. Same chair, same window, same framing every time — the sameness is the brand. Subtitles. Nothing else.
**Batch:** three in twenty minutes, every film block.
**Idea bank:** "Strong is not a size." · "Your knees don't hurt because you're old. They hurt because you stopped loading them." · "Recovery is training." · "Nobody at the gym is watching you. They're watching themselves." · "Discipline is a plan you don't renegotiate at 6am." · "'I don't want to be the oldest person in the gym.' Every third woman who books a call says it. You train at home, on your terms. Nobody's watching."

### F5 · THE LIFT — purpose: saves · cadence: weekly (a 5-part series, re-run quarterly)
**Example:** Shane Dorian, "morning activation" follow-along with rounds and reps in the caption — https://www.instagram.com/doriancoreperformance/reel/DcRBGa9oxQk/ — 7.6K likes / 171 comments.
**Structure (30–45s):**
- 0–3s HOOK — "Lift 1 of 5: the deadlift. Because you pick things up every day and nobody taught you how."
- 3–25s DEMO — Max does the lift, side angle, 3 reps. Two cues as on-screen text.
- 25–40s CUES — Max says the two cues to camera.
- CTA — "Save this. Comment STRONG for all five."
**Shots:** 5s low-angle wide of the set-up (feet, bar) · 8s side angle at hip height, Max, 3 reps · 3s close-up of grip · 10s cue to camera · 3s cinematic chalk. Sets and reps in the caption. Max demonstrates every lift himself.
**Note:** Clients send their own set-up clips through the app for form checks — those become F6 material with consent.
**The five:** deadlift · goblet squat · push (incline press) · row · loaded carry.

### F6 · LIFE PROOF — purpose: proof (no before/after bodies) · cadence: monthly or as clips come in
**Example:** Shane Dorian's surf-trip reel, "the pre-trip training paid off" — https://www.instagram.com/doriancoreperformance/reel/DbF6lPeIEu3/ — 17.3K likes / 1.0K comments, his top post. Training shown through the thing it was for.
**Structure (20–40s):**
- 0–3s HOOK — text on the clip: "She didn't train to look different. She trained to do this."
- 3–30s THE THING — client-filmed clip: the hike, the suitcase into the overhead bin, the grandchild, the ski day. Max voice-over, two sentences: name, age, what she trained.
- CTA — "What do you want to be strong for?"
**Shots:** 10–20s client phone clip (she sends it — ask on the weekly call) · 5s Max voice memo · optional 3s of her lifting, from her own set-up clip, as a cut-in.
**Source:** Everything here is self-filmed by clients on their phones and sent through the app or WhatsApp.
**Effort for Max:** near zero.

### F7 · THE KEYWORD — purpose: leads · cadence: every 2 weeks
**Example:** Harry, "Comment COACHING and I'll send a DM" — https://www.instagram.com/resurgent_harry/reel/DUstBDjDBC0/ — 794 likes / 30 comments on a 30-second, one-take reel.
**Structure (30s):**
- 0–3s HOOK — "If you're over 40 and you've never lifted, I made this for you."
- 3–20s WHAT IT IS — holds the printed guide; three things that are in it.
- 20–30s THE WORD — "Comment STRONG and I'll send it."
**Shots:** 20s talking head holding the guide · 5s close-up of the guide pages · 3s cinematic gym.
**Mechanics:** we reply to every comment with the link, by hand, within the hour → /start funnel page → email → intro call.
