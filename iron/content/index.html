Part one

The 7 formats

Each format is deconstructed the same way: Hook (0–3s) → Body → CTA, plus the exact shots it needs. Example posts are real, pulled 5 Sep 2026 with their engagement.

F1

The Reintroduction

TrustQuarterly · pinned
60–90sReel, or a 5-slide carousel
ExampleReal post, pulled 5 Sep 2026.
Based on
Harry Mackarnesscoach
“Allow me to reintroduce myself”
@resurgent_harry
4.3Klikes75comments

His top post of 2026. Text on a portrait, full origin story in the caption.

DeconstructionHook → Who → Why → Promise → CTA
0–3s
Hook

A line that contradicts what you see: “Most people cross the street when they see me. My clients are women in their fifties.”

3–30s
Who

Four facts, one line each: tattooed · licensed PT · 140+ women coached · 20 clients max.

30–60s
Why

The turn: why women 40+. One concrete moment, not a speech: his first client over fifty, terrified of the gym, deadlifting her bodyweight a year later. The past gets one calm sentence here, at most — “Before coaching, years in security work abroad. He left that life; this one is quieter.” Never on screen.

60–80s
Promise

What coaching is like: weekly video call, programme in the app, daily check-ins. Private, calm, no shouting.

Last 5s
CTA

“If that’s the kind of coach you want in your corner, the link in bio is a 20-minute call.”

ShotsOn screen: the hook line only. Sound: voice + low music.
4 ×10sTalking head · his gym or home gym, seated, tattoos visible
3sCinematic · empty gym
5sHands loading a bar
3sMax checking a training log
1 ×StillPortrait · cover
Max’s hooks

Most people cross the street when they see me. My clients are women in their fifties.

Allow me to reintroduce myself.

What he did in his twenties vs how he trains now, ending on why. A personal then/now that lands on a lesson.

DeconstructionHook → Then → Now → CTA
0–3s
Hook

The “then” as one line: “At 25 I trained to look dangerous.”

3–30s
Then

What he did and why. One concrete detail. Allowed to be funny.

30–50s
Now

What changed and why it matters for her: “Now I train so I can carry my daughter up four flights at seventy. So do my clients.”

End
CTA

Soft: “Follow for the next one.”

Shots
3 ×10sTalking head · his gym or home gym
40sOr: one walking take
3sCinematic detail · training log, chalk, the gym door
5sLow-angle of his own lift · the “now” visual
Max’s hooks

At 25 I trained to look dangerous. At 40 I train to be useful.

Two hours a day in my twenties. Three sessions a week now. Stronger.

Guardrail: his past is backstory, one sentence at most, never the hook.

F4

One Minute

Consistency3 per week · batch-filmed
45–60sOne thought, to camera
ExampleReal post, pulled 5 Sep 2026.
Based on
Xavier The Life Coachhis whole account is this format
“I almost gave up on my Instagram”
@xavierthelifecoach
5.4Klikes752comments

One thought, to camera, honest, 60 seconds.

DeconstructionHook → One idea → Landing → CTA
0–3s
Hook

The thought as one sentence: “You don’t need motivation. You need a Tuesday at seven that’s already booked.”

3–45s
One idea

Why, in plain words, one example. No second point.

45–55s
Landing

Say the line again.

End
CTA

None, or “save this”.

ShotsBatch: three in twenty minutes, every film block.
60sTalking head · same chair, same window, same framing every time — the sameness is the brand. Subtitles. Nothing else.
Max’s hook

You don’t need motivation. You need a Tuesday at seven that’s already booked.

Idea bankOne per film. Say it, explain it, say it again.
“Strong is not a size.” “Your knees don’t hurt because you’re old. They hurt because you stopped loading them.” “Recovery is training.” “Nobody at the gym is watching you. They’re watching themselves.” “Discipline is a plan you don’t renegotiate at 6am.” “‘I don’t want to be the oldest person in the gym.’ Every third woman who books a call says it. You train at home, on your terms. Nobody’s watching.”

3–30s
The thing

Client-filmed clip: the hike, the suitcase into the overhead bin, the grandchild, the ski day. Max voice-over, two sentences: name, age, what she trained.

She didn’t train to look different. She trained to do this.
