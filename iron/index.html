07FAQ

Straight answers.

If yours isn't here, ask it on the call. There's no question he hasn't heard and none he'll judge you for.

I haven't trained in 20 years.

Then you're the typical client. Muscle responds to training at every age — strength gains are documented in people starting in their seventies and eighties. We start with an assessment, not a workout, and build from what your body can do today.

Will I get bulky?

No. Building large muscle takes years of highly specific training, eating and hormones — a program this isn't. What you will notice: posture, stairs, sleep, energy, and a body that does what you ask of it. Strong is not a size.

I have a bad back or a bad knee.

Usually a reason to start, not to wait. The assessment finds what your joints tolerate and the program builds from there — in most cases, appropriate strength work is exactly what a back or a knee has been missing. Where medical clearance is needed first, he'll say so and work alongside your physiotherapist.

Is the studio private?

Completely. A standalone studio in Östermalm, not a corner of a commercial gym. When you train, the door is closed: no members, no walk-ins, no mirrors, no audience. Duo sessions are the only exception — with the person you chose to bring.

Do you weigh people?

No. There is no scale in the studio. Progress is measured in strength, grip, resting heart rate and blood pressure — the numbers that predict the next thirty years. Never bodyweight, never photos.
