# Max Sandell — Content System
*Cursus Capital · v1 · 2026-09-05 · Source of truth for the rendered page at /haven/content/*

## 0. What this is

A repeatable machine that turns Max's week into 5–6 posts a week without Max ever having to "think of content". Four parts:

1. **Format library** — 16 proven content formats, reverse-engineered from the 7 accounts Max sent us, then rebuilt for *his* persona (ex-military, close protection, tattooed, calm) and *his* client (women 40+).
2. **Shooting log** — one card per piece of content: hook, beats, shot list, location, duration. Max films what the card says. Nothing else.
3. **Weekly shooting schedule** — one 90-minute film block per week, batch-filmed. Sent Sunday evening.
4. **Content calendar** — Cursus edits, captions, schedules and reports. Max only films.

Division of labour: **Max films. Cursus does everything else.**

**The pattern is not subtle.** Follower count does not predict engagement. The three accounts with a strong point of view and a personal story (Paul, Shane, Xavier) get 2–4% of their audience engaging on a median post. The three accounts posting generic coach tips (Jason S., Jason B., Gary) get 0.04–0.13% — one-tenth to one-hundredth as much, despite bigger audiences. Gary's account is the cautionary tale: templated "coach for men 40+" content with meme-style hooks, listicles and "DM me READY" CTAs, and a median of 12 likes on 21K followers.

What actually performed, across all seven:

- **Story beats tips.** Origin stories, "then vs now", personal history. Harry's reintroduction post is his best post of the year. Paul's "every diet I've tried in 20 years" did 3.6K / 353 comments.
- **Contrarian lists explode the comments.** Paul's two "things that need to go" reels: 25.9K / 1.7K and 3.2K / 448. The CTA is always "What have I missed?" — the audience finishes the list.
- **Purpose beats aesthetics.** Shane trains *to surf*; his surf-trip reel (17.3K) outperforms every workout demo. Training for a life, not a body.
- **Simple evidence, one action.** Shane's "muscle loss starts in your 30s and speeds up after 40" — 3.6K / 108 as a plain photo post.
- **Follow-along routines get saved.** Shane's 20-minute "morning activation" with reps in the caption: 7.6K / 171.
- **Personality and humour.** Harry's military anecdote clip (waking up drunk before an 8-mile march): 3.1K / 55.
- **A daily consistency engine.** Xavier's "Daily Morning Minute Message" — one thought, 60 seconds, to camera. It's the format that keeps the account alive between the big posts.
- **Comment-keyword lead gen works.** Harry's "Comment COACHING and I'll DM you": 794 / 30 on a low-effort reel.

What did not work: tips without a person behind them, generic hooks ("Does this describe you?"), client results as abstract graphics, and anything that looks like a template.

## 2. Principles for Max's content

1. **He is the format.** A tattooed ex-close-protection officer who is the calmest, most patient person in the room is a story no other trainer in Stockholm can tell. Every piece should be un-copyable by someone without his background.
2. **Protector, never drill sergeant.** Close protection is about preparation, calm, and making the day boring for the client. That is the lens: "I plan so nothing goes wrong. That's what your program is."
3. **Speak to her, not at her.** The audience is women 40+. Address the fears we heard in Haven's research: intimidating gyms, diet-culture fatigue, "am I too old", "will I get hurt", "where do I even start."
4. **Strength, never shrinking.** No before/after bodies, no scale numbers, no "summer body". Proof is deadlifts, bone density, blood pressure, carrying grandkids, hiking.
5. **One idea per piece.** If a script needs a second point, it's two pieces.
6. **Batch, don't improvise.** One film block a week produces the whole week. Improvised content is what kills consistency.

## 3. Format library

Effort = Max's time. **Low** = filmed on a phone during normal work. **Medium** = 10–15 minutes in the film block. **High** = needs a client, a location or a second camera.

### Pillar A — Who Max is (trust)

**A1 · "Allow me to reintroduce myself"**
Inspired by @resurgent_harry (4.3K / 75 — his top post). An origin-story post, re-cut quarterly for new followers.
- What: 3–5 slide carousel (moody portrait + text) or a 60–90s talking-head reel. Military → close protection → why he now trains women 40+.
- Hooks: "I spent 12 years making sure nothing happened to the people I protected. Now I do the same for your strength." / "Most people cross the street when they see me. My clients are women in their fifties." / "Allow me to reintroduce myself."
- On-screen text: the hook line only, serif, small.
- Shot list: (1) slow push-in on Max seated in the studio, tattoos visible, calm; (2) hands wrapping a client's barbell; (3) empty studio at morning light; (4) still portrait for the carousel cover.
- Duration: 60–90s reel / 5 slides. Cadence: quarterly, pinned. Effort: medium. CTA: "If that sounds like the kind of coach you'd want in your corner, the link in bio is a 20-minute call."
- Why it works for her: it answers the question she's already asking when she sees him — *who is this man and why should I trust him?*

**A2 · "The protector's lens"**
Inspired by Harry's military anecdote clips (3.1K / 55) and Xavier's personal-crossover reels (4.5K / 600). One close-protection lesson, translated into training.
- What: 45–60s talking head or walking-and-talking outdoors.
- Hooks: "In close protection, a boring day is a perfect day. Same in training." / "The first thing you learn protecting people: never surprise the person you're protecting." / "Bodyguards don't wing it. Neither should your first session."
- Shot list: walking shot along Strandvägen or through Humlegården, phone at chest height; one cutaway of the studio door / Max checking a training log.
- Duration: 45–60s. Cadence: every 2 weeks. Effort: medium. CTA: soft ("Follow for the next one").
- Why it works: it makes his background an asset for *her* rather than something intimidating. Humour welcome; never war stories.

**A3 · "What she said on the first call"**
Inspired by @fitinmidlife's "midlife drift" story (602 / 46 — his best post despite 274K followers). Anonymised first-call fears, answered.
- What: 45s talking head. Max reads one thing a prospective client said, then answers it honestly.
- Hooks: "'I don't want to be the oldest person in the gym.' — every third woman who calls me." / "'I've never lifted anything heavier than my kids.' Good. That's heavier than you think." / "'Will you make me do burpees?' No. Never."
- Shot list: Max at the studio desk, phone on a small tripod, natural light; optional B-roll of the empty, quiet studio.
- Duration: 30–45s. Cadence: weekly. Effort: low. CTA: "Book the call. Say the scary thing out loud. It's usually smaller than you think."
- Why it works: she hears her own objection said out loud by someone who isn't judging it.

### Pillar B — Contrarian (reach)

**B1 · "Fitness advice for women over 40 that needs to go in the bin"**
Inspired by @barbells_and_mountains (25.9K / 1.7K and 3.2K / 448). A 5-item contrarian list, delivered walking or in the gym, items as on-screen text.
- What: 45–60s reel. Max lists 5 things, one line each, with a beat of silence between.
- Hooks: "Fitness advice for women over 40 that needs to go in the bin." / "Five things I'd ban from every gym if I could." / "'Toning' isn't a thing. Let's talk about that."
- Items bank: "toning" · light weights for high reps · "shrink for summer" · cardio as punishment · "you're too old to start lifting" · the 1,200-calorie diet · "just do Pilates" · fasted training for women in perimenopause · scale weigh-ins as the only metric.
- Shot list: walking shot (outdoors, moving background) OR standing in the rack, static. On-screen: numbered list appears item by item.
- Duration: 45–60s. Cadence: every 2 weeks. Effort: medium. CTA: **"What have I missed?"** (the audience finishes the list — this is where the comments come from).
- Guardrail: contrarian about the *industry*, never about *her*. Paul swears; Max doesn't need to.

**B2 · "I'm sick of…"**
Inspired by Paul's "I'm sick of 'what I eat in a day' posts" (1.1K / 118). One-topic mini rant.
- What: 30–45s, single idea, tighter than B1.
- Hooks: "I'm sick of 'what I eat in a day' posts. What you eat on one perfect day means nothing." / "I'm sick of seeing women told to eat less and move more at 48." / "I'm sick of the word 'toned'."
- Shot list: talking head, slightly closer than usual; one cutaway.
- Duration: 30–45s. Cadence: every 2 weeks (alternating with B1). Effort: low. CTA: "Agree? Send this to someone who needs to hear it."

**B3 · "Two truths"**
Inspired by @doriancoreperformance's evidence posts (3.6K / 108) and "the long game" (3.2K / 106). One fact, one action.
- What: text card (carousel, 2 slides) or 20s reel. Slide 1: the fact. Slide 2: what to do about it.
- Fact bank: muscle mass drops ~3–8% per decade after 30 and faster after menopause; bone density: strength training is the only exercise that reliably builds it; grip strength predicts longevity better than BMI; a woman who starts lifting at 50 can add strength for 20 years; protein needs go *up* after 40, not down.
- Shot list: none needed for the card; for reel — Max at the whiteboard in the studio.
- Duration: 2 slides / 20s. Cadence: weekly. Effort: low. CTA: "Save this for the next time someone tells you to eat less."
- Guardrail: cite the source in the caption. Never diagnose, never mention hormone therapy.

### Pillar C — Proof (without before/after)

**C1 · "Numbers that matter"**
Inspired by Gary's blood-results graphic — a good idea executed without story. Ours has the story.
- What: carousel or 30s reel. One client's *strength or health* number over time: deadlift 40 → 80 kg; grip strength; resting HR; blood pressure; bone density scan; stairs without stopping.
- Hooks: "Karin, 52. Deadlift in March: 40 kg. Deadlift today: 82.5 kg. Bodyweight: none of your business." / "This is the only 'before and after' I post."
- Shot list: the actual lift (phone, side angle, low), Max spotting; the number handwritten on the whiteboard.
- Duration: 30s. Cadence: every 2 weeks. Effort: medium (needs client consent). CTA: "This is what 12 weeks looks like when nobody's shouting at you."
- Guardrail: never bodyweight, never photos of her body as the point. First name + age only, with written consent.

**C2 · "Life proof"**
Inspired by Shane's surf-trip reel (17.3K / 1K) and Harry's travel posts (3.6K, 1.4K). Clients doing the thing they trained for.
- What: 20–40s reel from client-submitted phone clips: the hike, the ski trip, lifting the suitcase into the overhead bin, carrying a grandchild, moving house. Max voice-over, two sentences.
- Hooks: "She didn't train to look different. She trained to do this." / "Kebnekaise, 58 years old, no poles."
- Shot list: client clips (ask at check-in: "send me 10 seconds of the thing"); Max recording a voice memo.
- Duration: 20–40s. Cadence: monthly or as they come. Effort: low for Max (clients film). CTA: "What do you want to be strong *for*? Tell me below."

**C3 · "In her words"**
Client interview, one question only.
- What: 45–60s. Max asks a single question off-camera; she answers.
- Questions: "What were you afraid of before the first session?" / "What surprised you about Max?" / "What can you do now that you couldn't a year ago?"
- Shot list: client seated in the studio, warm light, phone on tripod at eye level, Max's voice off-camera. Cover frame: her mid-sentence, smiling.
- Duration: 45–60s. Cadence: monthly. Effort: high (client + scheduling). CTA: "She was nervous too. Link in bio."

### Pillar D — Train with Max (saves)

**D1 · "The 5 lifts every woman over 40 should own"**
Inspired by Shane's routine demos (7.6K / 171). One lift per reel; a five-part series; also the lead magnet for the funnel.
- What: 30–45s. A client (or Max) performs the lift; Max explains *why this lift* in one sentence, then the two cues that matter.
- The five: deadlift (hinge) · goblet squat · push (incline press or push-up) · pull (row) · loaded carry.
- Hooks: "Lift 1 of 5: the deadlift. Because you pick things up every day and nobody taught you how." / "The one exercise I'd give every woman over 40 if I could only give one."
- Shot list: side angle at hip height; a close-up of the set-up (feet, grip); Max's cue delivered to camera. Reps and sets in the caption.
- Duration: 30–45s. Cadence: weekly until the series is done, then re-run quarterly. Effort: medium. CTA: "Save this. Comment STRONG and I'll send you all five as a guide."

**D2 · "The morning five"**
Inspired by @fitinmidlife's 6-movement morning routine (128 / 14) and Shane's activation (7.6K / 171). A 4-minute follow-along.
- What: 45–60s time-lapse or cut-down of a 5-move routine, reps in caption.
- Hooks: "Four minutes before your coffee. That's it." / "If your back is stiff every morning, do this before you sit down."
- Shot list: living-room or studio floor, wide static shot, natural light, Max demonstrates each move for 8s.
- Duration: 45–60s. Cadence: every 2 weeks. Effort: low. CTA: "Save it, do it tomorrow, tell me how it felt."

**D3 · "Session snapshot"**
Cinematic B-roll of a real private session. Removes the fear of the unknown.
- What: 15–25s, no talking. Text overlay: what we did and why. Music, slow.
- Hooks (text): "Tuesday, 07:00. Ingrid, 61. Week 6. Today: hinge pattern, 3 sets, then coffee." / "This is what a first session actually looks like. No mirrors, no crowd, no shouting."
- Shot list: filmed by Max on his phone during sessions (with consent): the barbell being loaded, chalk, her hands, the log book, the studio door, coffee cups. Faces optional.
- Duration: 15–25s. Cadence: weekly. Effort: low. CTA: none, or "Questions about what a session is like? Ask below."
- Why it works: the #1 fear is *not knowing what happens in there*. This shows it — quiet, private, calm.

### Pillar E — Consistency engine

**E1 · "One minute with Max"**
Inspired by @xavierthelifecoach's Daily Morning Minute Message (2–5K per post on 99K). One thought, to camera, 60 seconds. Batch-filmed: 6–8 in one sitting.
- What: 45–60s talking head. One idea. No editing beyond subtitles and a cover.
- Idea bank: "Strong is not a size." / "You don't need motivation, you need a Tuesday 07:00 that's already booked." / "Your knees don't hurt because you're old. They hurt because you stopped loading them." / "Recovery is training." / "The gym isn't scary. Strangers are. There are none here." / "Discipline is just a plan you don't renegotiate at 6am."
- Shot list: same seat, same light, same framing every time — the consistency *is* the brand. Phone on tripod, eye level, slightly below.
- Duration: 45–60s. Cadence: 3 per week. Effort: low (all 3 filmed in 20 minutes). CTA: rotate — none / "follow" / "save".

**E2 · "Words on the wall"**
Inspired by @jasonbrowntraining's branded text cards (his best-performing format: 187–224 on 77K). A single aphorism in the Haven visual system.
- What: one image. Ivory ground, Marcellus serif, one line, small monogram.
- Line bank: "Strong for the long run." / "You are not too old. You are under-trained." / "Protect your strength like it's someone you love." / "Nobody is watching you at the gym. They're watching themselves." / "Toned is just strong, whispered."
- Cadence: 2 per week. Effort: zero (Cursus produces). CTA: none.

### Pillar F — Conversion

**F1 · "Comment STRONG"**
Inspired by Harry's "Comment COACHING and I'll DM you" (794 / 30). Comment-keyword lead gen, wired to the funnel.
- What: 30s reel: Max holds up the printed "5 lifts" guide, says what it is, says the word.
- Hooks: "If you're over 40 and you've never lifted, I made this for you. Comment STRONG and I'll send it." / "Five lifts. One page. Free. Comment STRONG."
- Mechanics: ManyChat (or Meta's native automation) replies to the keyword with the guide link → /start funnel page → email captured → intro-call CTA.
- Duration: 30s. Cadence: every 2 weeks. Effort: low. CTA: the keyword.

**F2 · "This is for you if / not for you if"**
Qualification post. Harry's "Comment COACHING" reel did this well; Gary's "Does this describe you?" did it without personality (5 / 4).
- What: 45s talking head or 6-slide carousel. Three "for you" lines, three "not for you" lines.
- For you if: you haven't trained in years and you're nervous about it · you want to be strong for the next 30 years, not thin for the next 30 days · you want one calm person in your corner.
- Not for you if: you want a 6-week transformation · you want someone to shout at you · you want to be weighed every week.
- Cadence: monthly. Effort: low. CTA: "Free intro call. Link in bio. No hard sell — I'm bad at it."

## 9. Guardrails

- No before/after body photos. No scale numbers. No "summer body", "toned", "shred", "bikini".
- No client identifiable without written consent; first name + age only.
- No medical claims. Facts are cited in the caption. Never mention HRT or supplements by brand.
- Military: lessons and calm, never combat stories, never anything that dramatises violence.
- Tone: calm, dry, kind. Max never shouts, never points at the camera, never uses the word "ladies".
- Every piece must pass one question: *would a nervous 52-year-old feel safer after watching this?*
