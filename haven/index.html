Why strength, why now

Strength is the quiet foundation of everything after 40.

This isn't about aesthetics — it's about the physiology of the decades ahead. The research is remarkably consistent, and remarkably hopeful: it responds at any age.

~1%
Muscle per year

From around 40, muscle mass declines roughly 1% a year if untrained. Strength training doesn't just slow it — it reverses it.

Bone
Density & resilience

Loaded, progressive movement signals bone to rebuild — one of the most effective known responses to declining bone density.

Joints
Support, not strain

Strong muscle around a joint is its best protection. Knees and backs usually don't need rest — they need intelligent strength.

Energy
Daily capacity

More muscle means better blood-sugar control, deeper sleep, and more usable energy for the life you actually live.

Grip strength and leg strength are among the strongest predictors of healthy, independent longevity. In other words: this is the work that keeps you carrying your own suitcase at 80.

Honest answers

The questions everyone asks first.

If yours isn't here, ask it on the intro call — there's no question Max hasn't heard, and none he'll judge you for.

I haven't trained in 20 years. Is it too late?

Not even close. Muscle responds to training at every age — studies show meaningful strength gains in people starting in their 60s, 70s and beyond. Twenty years away just means we start gently and build carefully. Most clients are surprised how quickly their body remembers.

Will I get bulky?

No — and it's a kind question to answer honestly. Building large muscle takes years of highly specific training, eating and hormones that this program isn't designed for. What you'll actually notice: better posture, easier stairs, steadier energy, and clothes fitting the way you'd like. Strong looks like you, only more capable.

I have a bad back / dodgy knee. Can I still train?

Usually, yes — carefully and intelligently. The movement assessment exists precisely for this: we find what your body tolerates well and build from there. In most cases, appropriate strength work is what backs and knees have been waiting for. Where medical clearance is needed first, Max will tell you straight and work alongside your physiotherapist.

Is the studio really private?

Completely. It's a standalone studio in Östermalm — not a corner of a commercial gym. When you train, the door is closed and the hour is yours: no other members, no walk-ins, no audience. Duo sessions are the only exception, and only with the person you chose to bring.
