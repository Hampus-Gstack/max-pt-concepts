I. Philosophy

The quiet discipline of doing less, better.

Most training fails not from lack of effort, but from noise — borrowed programs, borrowed goals, and a pace set by someone else's life. My work begins by removing what does not belong to you.

What remains is deliberately simple: a handful of movements executed with precision, loads that rise on evidence rather than enthusiasm, and recovery treated as seriously as the work itself. Progress kept this way does not need to be defended. It compounds.

I coach a small number of clients at a time, in private facilities across Stockholm and remotely for those abroad. Every program is written for one person only — you.

Excellence is not an event. It is a standard, kept daily, in private.
— Max

III. The Method
No. 01

Assessment

A two-hour private consultation: movement screening, health history, and an honest conversation about what you want your body to be capable of.

No. 02

Architecture

Your program is written from a blank page — training, nutrition, and recovery designed around your physiology and your calendar, not a template.

No. 03

Execution

Coached sessions where every repetition is observed and refined. Load rises only when the data — not the mood — says it should.

No. 04

Refinement

Quarterly reviews measured against your original brief. The program evolves; the standard does not.
